Missable warning - read before you play

13 trophies are flagged missable: the named story missions Trust, Break the Bank, Family Matters, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked and Snow Storm; the strand finales for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone; Positive Mental Attitude, where one meditation spot is reported missable; and Actions Speak Louder, where choices are permanent. No mission replay has been reported in the sources checked, so keep rotating manual saves and finish each of these when it is offered.

Roadmap to the platinum

  1. Stage 1 - Story and side strands first. Play the story chapters and missions in order and finish every stranger strand finale as it appears. This clears the missable trophies while they are available.
  2. Stage 2 - Activities and skills. Stunts, shooting range golds, Top Fun wins, races, challenges and the partner and robbery trophies.
  3. Stage 3 - Collectibles and exploration. Card sets, WAiNK locations, meditation spots, discovered vehicles and full exploration of Leonida.
  4. Stage 4 - Completion and the 100% grind. Money trophies, garage properties and the push to 100 percent completion.
  5. Stage 5 - Secrets and platinum cleanup. The hidden, vaguely described secret trophies, then the platinum itself once the other 54 are earned.
